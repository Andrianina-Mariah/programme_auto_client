A. Ajouter un patient (POST)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/patients \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{
  "nom": "Rakoto",
  "prenom": "Jean",
  "dateNaissance": "2000-05-15",
  "sexe": "Masculin",
  "telephone": "0341234567",
  "email": "jean@gmail.com"
}'

B. Récupérer tous les patients (GET)
curl http://localhost:8080/api/patients

C. Récupérer un patient par ID (GET)
curl http://localhost:8080/api/patients/1

D. Modifier un patient (PUT)
curl -X PUT http://localhost:8080/api/patients/4 \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{
  "nom": "Rakoto",
  "prenom": "Jean Pierre",
  "dateNaissance": "2000-05-15",
  "sexe": "Masculin",
  "telephone": "0347654321",
  "email": "jeanpierre@gmail.com"
}'

E. Supprimer un patient (DELETE)
curl -i -X DELETE http://localhost:8080/api/patients/5

curl -i http://localhost:8080/api/patients/999
curl -i http://localhost:8080/api/inconnue
